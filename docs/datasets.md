# Dataset Specifications & Provenance

HydroEventLab integrates community-standardized, openly licensed hydrological datasets:

## 1. Caravan v1.6 (Primary Benchmark)

- **Official Repository**: https://github.com/kratzert/Caravan
- **Zenodo Distribution**: https://zenodo.org/records/15529786, https://zenodo.org/records/15530021
- **DOI**: [10.1038/s41597-023-01975-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-01975-w)
- **Citation**: Kratzert, F., Nearing, G., Addor, N. et al. Caravan - A global community dataset for large-sample hydrology. _Scientific Data_ 10, 61 (2023).
- **Attributes Included**:
  - Catchment boundaries & gauge coordinates.
  - Drainage area ($km^2$), mean elevation ($m$), mean slope ($m/km$).
  - Mean precipitation ($mm/day$), PET ($mm/day$), aridity index, snow fraction.
  - Land cover fractions (forest, crops, urban).
  - In-situ gauge streamflow normalized by catchment area ($mm/day$).
  - Catchment-averaged ERA5-Land daily precipitation, temperature, and potential evapotranspiration.

## 2. GRDC-Caravan (Community Extension)

- **Zenodo Distribution**: https://zenodo.org/records/15349031
- **DOI**: [10.5194/essd-17-4613-2025](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-17-4613-2025)
- **Citation**: Lehner et al. GRDC-Caravan: extending global large-sample hydrology benchmarks. _Earth System Science Data_ 17, 4613-2025 (2025).
- **Licensing**: Open subset compliant with CC-BY 4.0.
