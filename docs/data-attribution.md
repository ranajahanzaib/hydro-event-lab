# Data Attribution & Licensing

HydroEventLab relies upon open scientific data contributed by the international hydrological community.

## Caravan Dataset

- **Release**: Caravan v1.6
- **DOI**: [10.1038/s41597-023-01975-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-01975-w)
- **Zenodo**: Records 15529786 and 15530021
- **Citation**:
  > Kratzert, F., Nearing, G., Addor, N., Bloom, T., Lusser, E., Gauch, M., & Klotz, D. (2023). Caravan - A global community dataset for large-sample hydrology. _Scientific Data_, 10(1), 61.
- **Underlying Regional Data**:
  - CAMELS (US): USGS streamflow & NLDAS/Daymet forcing (Newman et al., 2015; Addor et al., 2017).
  - CAMELS-GB: National River Flow Archive (Coxon et al., 2020).
  - CAMELS-AUS: Bureau of Meteorology (BoM) (Fowler et al., 2021).
  - CAMELS-BR: Agência Nacional de Águas (Chagas et al., 2020).
  - CAMELS-CL: Center for Climate and Resilience Research (Alvarez-Garreton et al., 2018).
  - LamaH-CE: Large-Sample Data for Hydrology and Environmental Sciences for Central Europe (Klingler et al., 2021).
  - HYSETS: Database of Canadian and American Hydrometeorological Basins (Arsenault et al., 2020).
